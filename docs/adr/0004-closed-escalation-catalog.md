# Escalation is a closed catalog

The Assistant pauses only for: engine-required human (high-value, inspection, unusable identity), delivered-but-missing, fraud, a demanded policy exception after a lawful refusal, privacy beyond own profile, and product technical assistance. Identity means an active Customer record after portal email login — not a chat challenge (CPF/email). A coded refusal (outside window, final sale, already shipped) is explained in-thread; it becomes Escalation only if the Customer insists on an exception. Unknown intent, low classifier confidence, Order Fact lookup, generic help with an Order, and a failed Order lookup are not in the catalog.
