# TechStore Warranty Policy

This policy can override the standard 30-day refund window for defects.

1. Default warranty is 90 days from delivery.
2. Laptops have a 365-day warranty.
3. A defective product inside warranty may be refunded or replaced even after the 30-day refund window.
4. Defective claims after 7 days on electronics require inspection.
5. Warranty does not apply to damage from misuse. Escalate ambiguous cases.
