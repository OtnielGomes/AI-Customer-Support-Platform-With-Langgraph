# NexaCommerce Shipping Policy

1. Cancellation is allowed only before the shipment leaves the warehouse.
2. After `shipped`, `in_transit`, or `delivered`, the customer must use the return flow instead of cancel.
3. A shipment marked delivered when the customer did not receive it is a missing-delivery case, not a preference return.
4. Delayed shipments (ETA before today, still in transit) are tracked; they are not automatic refunds.
5. Carriers: Correios, Jadlog, DHL, FedEx.
